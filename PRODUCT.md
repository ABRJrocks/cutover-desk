# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineering leads and release coordinators rehearsing a deployment or migration at a laptop during a team planning session. The primary task is deciding which step is ready, who owns it and what evidence permits the next step.

## Product Purpose

Turn a dependency-aware cutover plan into a human-operated rehearsal, capture decisions and export a portable handoff record. Success means a user can build a plan, see its critical path, rehearse a failure and recovery, then share the plan and results without creating an account.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, practical. A restrained white-and-charcoal workbench with a small burnt-orange accent, confirmed by the repository owner.

## Anti-references

Decorative gradients, glowing panels, endless metric cards and ornamental dashboard chrome. Avoid implying that local notes are verified approvals or that rehearsal results establish production readiness.

## Design Principles

- Put the next available action beside the work it affects.
- Explain blockers with the dependency that caused them.
- Distinguish a plan, a human observation and an automated check.
- Preserve the record before inviting a new rehearsal.
- Make export and recovery first-class workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA through readable contrast, semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, labeled inputs and status text alongside color. Respect reduced motion. Verify the main flows with browser tests and an automated accessibility scan, without claiming a complete conformance audit.
